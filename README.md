Batly Privacy Policy
Effective date: October 7, 2026

Batly is a menu bar app for macOS that warns you when your battery gets low. This policy explains what Batly does with your information. The short version: nothing leaves your Mac.

Data we collect
None. Batly does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. Batly has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party code.

Internet access
Batly cannot connect to the internet. The app is sandboxed by macOS and does not have network permission, so it is technically unable to send anything anywhere.

What stays on your Mac
Battery level and charging state: Batly reads these from macOS to decide when to warn you. They are used only while the app is running and are never saved or sent anywhere.
Your settings (alert levels, sounds, message box, language): stored only on your Mac, in the app's own preferences. Deleting Batly's preferences or the app removes them.
Start at login: if you choose it, macOS remembers it in System Settings › General › Login Items. You can turn it off there at any time.
Batly does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, files, photos or screen contents, and it never asks for any of these permissions.

Children
Batly collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes
If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
